One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday, 24 November 2026,
as a newspaper front page:

- **Strait of Hormuz (lead story):** four oil tankers hit by naval mines
  and catch fire in the Strait of Hormuz, as Iran reports the attacks and
  refuses talks until its conditions are met.
- **US verdict:** a US jury finds Huawei guilty of trade-secret theft and
  bank fraud (acquitted of racketeering).
- **NVIDIA:** Jensen Huang is reinstated as CEO less than two weeks after
  resigning; Nvidia shares jump more than 30%.
- **Also:** a Russian overnight strike kills two and wounds 40 in
  Zaporizhzhia, Ukraine, and an Israeli airstrike kills Hamas internal
  security chief Naeem Abu Nahel in Gaza.

`main.ts` copies the finished 1024×1024 `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`). No network, no dependencies.
